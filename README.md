## 📌 Overview
**Stranger Things** is a *science fiction* and **horror** series set in the 1980s.  
It follows a group of kids who discover secret experiments and supernatural events in their town.

## 🎭 Main Characters 

- **Eleven** – girl with powers  
- **Mike Wheeler** – leader of the group  
- **Dustin Henderson** – funny and smart  
- **Lucas Sinclair** – brave friend  
- **Will Byers** – boy who disappears
